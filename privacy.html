Quartets

Privacy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 (version 1.1)

Quartets collects no personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app has no server of its own.

On your iPhone

Boards in progress, finished boards, Allegro runs, your streak, stats and settings stay on your iPhone. The widget reads your streak and today's progress from a shared container on the same device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Game Center (optional)

Game Center is off until you turn it on. If you do, Apple's Game Center receives your Allegro score, your longest streak and the laurels you earn, under your Game Center profile. That exchange is between your device and Apple and follows Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive it.

Purchases

The Season Ticket (subscription or lifetime) is sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the app only asks the App Store whether your ticket is active.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning bell, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing goes to a server.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

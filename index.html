Sixteen words sit on the board. They split into four groups of four, and each group shares a link: kinds of pasta, words that go before "ball", words with an animal hidden inside. Find all four groups before you make four mistakes.

Questions

What does "One away" mean?
Three of the four words you picked belong together. Swap one word and try again.
Does picking the same four words twice cost a mistake?
No. If you submit a set you already tried, the app tells you and nothing is counted.
What do the colors mean?
They show difficulty, from easiest to trickiest: green, orange, red and brown. Each one also has its own shape (circle, triangle, square, diamond), so you don't need to tell colors apart.
What's Hard mode?
Group names stay hidden until the puzzle is over. You still see which words went together. Turn it on in Settings.
When does a new puzzle come out?
At midnight on your phone's clock. Everyone gets the same puzzle for the same date.
How does the streak work?
Finish the day's puzzle, win or lose, to keep your streak going. Archive and pack puzzles don't count. A streak freeze covers one missed day a week (two with Unlimited).
What's free?
The daily puzzle, the last 7 days of the archive, the Starter pack, your streak, basic stats and the widget. Unlimited adds every past Daily, all packs (including new ones), full stats and extra themes.
How do I cancel a subscription?
On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Quartets. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Lifetime is a one-time payment and never renews.
I bought Unlimited on another device.
Open Settings in Quartets and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
